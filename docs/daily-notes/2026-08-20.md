The engineering improvements were useful: the project now catches leakage,
stale evidence, missing costs, source/runtime mismatches, execution-quality
failures, and misleading operator messages. But those protections established
that the strategy itself lacks demonstrated edge. Adjusting until a historical
slice looks profitable would invalidate the evidence process.
