# 2026-09-05 — Potter v3: the method as taught, measured; retirement stands

## What happened

Jake's diagnosis was that the project never used the Potter Box the way its
author uses it. The primary sources on disk confirmed that in detail
(`scanner/research/potter_v3/METHOD_SPEC_FROM_SOURCES.md`, 60 rules with
transcript line citations): the retired detector read regular-session daily
bars when he calls the 24-hour extended-hours candle "imperative"; it could
only fire on a close outside the box, when the cost-basis "punch back" is the
trigger he calls "the entirety of everything that I do"; it held five bars
with no target, when he trades box to box with a 50% trim, sells the next
morning, and stops on a 24-hour close back inside; it measured the stock path
when every return he quotes is an option contract; and its empty-space scorer
returned zero on 2,340 of 2,344 detector-passing setups because it treated
the box's own wicks as resistance and turned "nothing above" into a target
below the entry (`CODE_GAP_MAP.md`, section 0).

So a source-faithful research path was built beside the retired one
(`scanner/potter_v3/`, 54 tests, never touches the retired pipeline or the
retirement marker), pre-registered with one primary cell and committed before
any outcome was read, then run on 55 names over 512 extended-hours sessions
(2024-08-26 to 2026-09-04).

## Result

FAIL on every pre-registered gate (`scanner/research/potter_v3/RESULTS.md`):

| primary cell: bullish punch back, 3 sessions | value | gate |
|---|---:|---:|
| tradeable, non-overlapping signals / entry days | 62 / 56 | >= 60 days |
| mean net return (25 bps/side) | -0.47% | |
| HAC t of entry-day means | -1.45 | >= 2.0 |
| paired difference vs same-day control | +0.41%, t -0.08 | t >= 2.0 |
| day-clustered precision lower bound | 0.32 | >= 0.45 |
| modelled contract, mean net return | -18% | (reported) |

All 39 cells (13 trigger families x 3 horizons) are net negative on the stock
leg; gross returns sit near zero, so the round-trip cost floor is the whole
loss. Bearish cells are the worst (punch-back bear -1.3%, t -2.5), as in
every earlier study. The same-day drift controls are as negative as the
triggers, so the boxes add nothing over the day's market. The contract leg
(cheap out-of-the-money, 10-day, target-based) loses 15-40% on average once
theta and a 10%-per-side spread are charged. One secondary cell
(floor_reclaim at 1 session) beats its control at t +2.17, which is the one
or two such cells 39 evaluations produce by chance.

## Decision

The retirement stands; nothing was loosened and nothing will be re-run with
different constants. Revival criterion 1 has now been tested on the most
credible "genuinely new mechanism" available, the method itself, and it did
not pass. Remaining untested ideas are not parameter changes but different
inputs the project does not have: his lower-timeframe (1h/4h) entries with
intraday management, real option chains instead of a Black-Scholes proxy, and
the discretionary chart reading he describes as "every chart needs your
discretion".
