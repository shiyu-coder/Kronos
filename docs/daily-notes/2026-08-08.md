## Session 2 - transaction costs made first-class in the evidence gates

### What prompted it

An operator health check asked for the bot's trade history and P&L, and for "major adjustments" now that enough data has accumulated. The first two answers are the important ones:

- **There is no trade history and no P&L.** All 1,123 journal rows carry `final_pass=false`; `mode` is only `research_scan` (1,005) or `dry_run` (118), never `live`. The scanner has never placed a trade, which is the intended fail-closed behaviour, not a fault.
- **The accumulated data does not support strategy tuning.** The 44 resolved rows carrying a full outcome record average +0.0049 R at a standard deviation of 1.01 - a t-stat of 0.03. That is indistinguishable from zero, and it is the same null the 48-cell sprint, the Kronos backfill, and the chart-pattern sweep already returned. Tuning entry logic against it would be fitting noise.

The defensible adjustment was therefore not a new feature or a loosened gate. It was the one the 2026-07-10 sprint already identified as the next lever and which had never been wired into production: the cost model.

### The defect

`walk_triple_barrier` stores outcomes gross - it charges nothing. `return_pct`, `r_multiple`, and the win/loss `label` were all free-execution numbers, and every readiness gate consumed them: rank IC, average R, Wilson-LB precision, the entry-day HAC t-stats, tercile lift.

The only cost-aware value anywhere in the pipeline was `average_return_pct_after_slippage`, computed from a hardcoded `slippage_pct=0.05` (5 bps total) at `main.py:1644`. No gate read it. It was a reporting field.

So the go-live gates were measuring an edge that no execution could capture, at a cost assumption 10x below the 25 bps/side where the sprint's own `e4_decision` analysis showed this cohort's expectancy dying. A gate that reads 0 bps can only ever err toward promoting, which is the one direction a fail-closed scanner must not fail in.

### The change

- `EDGE_COST_BPS_PER_SIDE` (default 25 bps/side, `KRONOS_COST_BPS_PER_SIDE` to override) - the pre-registered sprint basis.
- `apply_transaction_costs` charges the round trip against every row BEFORE any metric is computed, re-divides by the stored `risk_pct_used` so net R stays on the R scale, and recomputes the win/loss label from the net return so precision is net too. Rows with no usable stop distance keep their stored R rather than inventing a denominator.
- Gross values are preserved as `gross_*` / `average_gross_return_pct`; the report stamps a `cost_model` provenance block.
- New audit check `costs_charged` fails closed when that block is missing or zero, and is ANDed into both `ranking_passed` and `threshold_passed`. Zeroing the env var cannot open a gate, and reports predating the cost model cannot either.
- `risk_pct_used` is now carried onto validation candidates.

No threshold was relaxed and no condition removed; the gate changes are additive AND-clauses only.

### What charging costs revealed

Bullish - the one direction the system was closest to promoting, and the direction all four of Friday's counterfactuals sit in - is negative once costs are charged. 37 of 1,500 gross wins are net losses. A new `bullish_edge_negative` warning is now raised alongside the existing bearish one.

Readiness stays `blocked` either way, so no live behaviour changed today. What changed is that a future run can no longer pass a gate on gross expectancy.
